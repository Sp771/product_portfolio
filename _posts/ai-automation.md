## Product

As Product Owner of MemberPlex at Aura Innovative Technology, my core job is owning the product: the roadmap, the backlog, UX/UI design, QA, and releases. Along the way I saw an opportunity to improve not just *what* we build, but *how* we build it. I took the initiative to introduce AI-driven tools, automations, and process improvements that make the whole team more efficient, organized, and productive.

![Where AI fits in the MemberPlex delivery cycle](/assets/posts/ai-automation/example.svg)

---

## My Role
* **🧠 AI Initiative Lead:** Identified where AI and automation could remove repetitive work across the company, then introduced and championed the tools and workflows to make it happen.

* **🧠 Backlog Owner:** Brought AI into backlog reviews to evaluate tickets, surface gaps in requirements, improve prioritization, and streamline refinement.

* **🧠 QA & Release Owner:** Designed AI-driven QA workflows and automated parts of the release process, while continuing to own testing, release validation, and sign-off.

* **🧠 Team Liaison:** Served as the bridge between leadership and the development team, using better tooling to give everyone clearer visibility into priorities and progress.

---

## Execution

**AI-assisted backlog management.** I introduced AI into our backlog reviews to help evaluate tickets, identify gaps in requirements before they reach development, and support clearer prioritization. Refinement sessions became faster and more focused.

**Standup and priority tracking.** I developed tools and automations that improve visibility into developer workloads, daily standup updates, ticket priorities, and overall project progress, so leadership and the team are always working from the same picture.

**Production release management.** I improved and automated parts of our release management process, creating more consistent deployment procedures, better release tracking, and clearer communication around production readiness across client staging and production environments.

**AI-assisted QA.** I am exploring and implementing AI-driven QA workflows that reduce repetitive manual testing and accelerate test preparation and validation, with the goal of shortening QA turnaround without compromising quality.

**GenAI with Amazon Bedrock.** I am leading an initiative that uses AWS-hosted models through Amazon Bedrock to automate feedback summarization and RFP response generation.

---

## Impact

* **🏅 Less administrative work:** Automations take over repetitive tracking, reporting, and testing tasks, freeing the team to spend more time on development and product improvements.

* **🏅 Clearer visibility:** Leadership and developers share a consistent, up-to-date view of workloads, priorities, and release readiness.

* **🏅 More consistent releases:** Standardized, partly automated deployment procedures make releases more predictable for both the team and our clients.

* **🏅 Company-wide reach:** These initiatives extend beyond MemberPlex, improving how teams across the company operate.
